Your Account

Welcome to your personal dashboard! Please feel free to get in touch with us using the contact form if you have any problems - if you forgot your password, username, email and more. All you need is proof of ownership of the account to ensure customer safety.

If you would like to delete your account, you can do so by getting in contact with us using our contact form. If you want to request a refund, change an order, you can do all of that here too, but please consider that some changes you would like to make to concurrent orders depends on if the order has already been shipped and is in transit with one of our drivers, meaning you may not be able to refund or edit the order.

Loyalty Scheme

As part of our loyalty scheme, we provide special offers for customers who are loyal to Greefield Local Hub. We appreciate our customers and we put your experience at the forefront of our innovation. Each order grants you a set amount of loyalty points equal to 5% of your total spend on each order.

You will accumulate more loyalty points as you place orders; you can use these points to get discounts at your next checkout, or whenever you decide to use them.
